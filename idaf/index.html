Index of Dental Anxiety and Fear

LinkML schema for defining data elements used in the IDAF-4C+ assessment instrument.

URI: https://w3id.org/OHD/DFA-study/idaf

Name: idaf_schema

Classes

Class Description

Slots

Slot Description
idaf_1_a (1a) I feel anxious shortly before going to the dentist.
idaf_1_b (1b) I generally avoid going to the dentist because I find the experience unpleasant or distressing.
idaf_1_c (1c) I get nervous or edgy about upcoming dental visits.
idaf_1_d (1d) I think that something really bad would happen to me if I were to visit a dentist.
idaf_1_e (1e) I feel afraid or fearful when visiting the dentist.
idaf_1_f (1f) My heart beats faster when I go to the dentist.
idaf_1_g (1g) I delay making appointments to go to the dentist.
idaf_1_h (1h) I often think about all the things that might go wrong prior to going to the dentist.
idaf_2_a (2a) Going to the dentist is actively avoided or else endured with intense fear or anxiety
idaf_2_b (2b) My fear of going to the dentist has been present for at least 6 month
idaf_2_c (2c) My fear, anxiety or avoidance of going to the dentist significantly affects my life in some way (dental pain, avoiding eating some foods, embarrassed or self-conscious about appearance of teeth or mouth, etc.)
idaf_2_d (2d) I am afraid of going to the dentist because I am concerned I may have a panic attack (abrupt fear with sweating, pounding liteart, fear of dying or losing control, chest pain etc.)
idaf_2_e (2e) I am afraid of going to the dentist because I am generally highly self-conscious or concerned about being watched or judged in social situations.
idaf_3_a (3a) Painful or uncomfortable procedures
idaf_3_b (3b) Feeling embarrassed or ashamed
idaf_3_c (3c) Not being in control of what is happening
idaf_3_d (3d) Feeling sick, queasy or disgusted
idaf_3_e (3e) Numbness caused by the anesthetic
idaf_3_f (3f) Not knowing what the dentist is going to do
idaf_3_g (3g) The cost of dental treatment
idaf_3_h (3h) Needles or injections
idaf_3_i (3i) Gagging or choking
idaf_3_j (3j) Having an unsympathetic or unkind dentist
